Questions

Capital investor FAQ

What backs the (N) token in this model?

First, 100% owned real estate: income-generating multifamily apartment portfolios bought with 100% equity and no debt. A $1,000,000 property value = $1,000,000 in value backing the tokens. Second, physical gold reserves, built from one-third of monthly NOI deposited into the gold storage account, alongside one-third deposited into the real estate reserve account. That dual backing — real estate first, then gold — is the core of the model.

Is there a minimum or maximum buy-in?

No — there is no minimum or maximum buy-in. This page does not collect money — contact Project Nimble directly at Info@ProjectNimble.US or 561-984-4565 to enquire.

What is the presale price and how many tokens are available?

The presale price is $0.01 per token, with 50 million tokens available at presale from the fixed 500 million supply. It is an early-adopter incentive, and presale proceeds fund Project Nimble's startup and operating costs only. Once those 50 million tokens are sold, the presale window closes and the price goes to $0.50 per token. As an illustrative example only of the price maths: $1 million at $0.01 = 100 million Nimble tokens. That example is not a buy-in amount, tier, minimum, or maximum.

How many tokens are there — will more be created?

500 million (N), fixed. No additional tokens will ever be minted. The stated allocation is 78% investors (390M), 10% founder (50M), 5% team (25M), 4% partnership (20M) and 3% pre-seed (15M).

Where does the rental income go?

Net operating income (NOI) is split one-third each to the gold storage account, the real estate reserve account, and the Nimble platform for operations. The gold storage and real estate reserve thirds are deposited monthly and form ongoing backing for the tokens; the platform third funds operations.

Does holding tokens give me equity or a share of profits?

No. (N) is a utility and settlement instrument for ecosystem liquidity, platform usage, and access to platform services, including property acquisition workflows, settlement mechanisms, and collateralizing operational functions. Holding (N) does not convey equity, company profit participation, dividends, or claims on company revenues.
