How the investment is allocated

Three complementary components, one third each.

The illustrative Nimble framework allocates one-third to real estate, one-third to gold reserves and one-third to Nimble coins. Final terms are governed by the investment documents.

1/3 / REAL ESTATE

Multifamily assets

Income-oriented residential properties form the operating base of the portfolio concept.

1/3 / GOLD

Gold reserves

A proposed reserve component intended to add a tangible store-of-value dimension.

1/3 / NIMBLE COINS

Nimble coins

A digital participation instrument whose rights, pricing and risks are defined in the governing documents.

Illustrative capital investment

$1 million invested financially.
