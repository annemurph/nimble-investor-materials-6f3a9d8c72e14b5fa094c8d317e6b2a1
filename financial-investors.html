Nimble company model

Real estate and gold are intended to support the token.

Wholly owned apartment properties provide the first stated source of value backing. Property NOI is then allocated among gold storage, real estate reserves and Project Nimble operations.

1/3 / REAL ESTATE RESERVES

Property reserves

One-third of NOI from company-owned properties goes to the real estate reserve account.

1/3 / GOLD

Gold storage

One-third of property NOI goes to the gold storage account.

1/3 / OPERATIONS

Nimble platform

One-third of property NOI supports Project Nimble’s platform and operations.
