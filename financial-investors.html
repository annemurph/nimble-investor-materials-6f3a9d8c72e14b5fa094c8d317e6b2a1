Nimble company model

The Nimble token is backed by real estate and physical gold.

Wholly owned apartment properties establish the token’s real-estate backing. One-third of property NOI continually purchases and stores physical gold, expanding the token’s gold backing over time.

1/3 / REAL ESTATE RESERVES

Strengthen the portfolio

1/3 / GOLD

Expand gold backing

One-third of property NOI purchases and stores physical gold that backs the Nimble token.

1/3 / OPERATIONS

Build the platform

One-third of property NOI funds Project Nimble’s platform and operations.
